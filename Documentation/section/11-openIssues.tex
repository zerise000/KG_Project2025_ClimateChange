\section{Open Issues}
As we saw during the whole procedure it is clear that the knowledge graph needs to be
integrated with more data, in particular it is necessary to include all the metereological
stations spread across the Trentino region and the locations of the different valleys and
territories. As mentioned in the purpose formalization phase one of the major obstacles about
the data retrievement has been the impossibility to scrape the meteotrentino dataset due to the
fact that the download links were dynamically generated, making the manual retrievement the only
possible solution; to fix this problem other datasets must be consulted in order to cover a wider
range over the region, or at least find a way to automatize the scraping process from the resources
illustrated in this report. Also the data about the geography of the single locations suffered of
a similar problem, which requires a further extension of the Knowledge Graph through external resources.
